Lost money to a scam? Act fast

The sooner you report, the better the chance your money can be frozen before the scammer moves it.

  1. Call the cyber fraud helpline: 1930

    National Cyber Crime helpline. Report the transaction as early as possible.

    📞 Call 1930

  2. File a complaint online

    Register at cybercrime.gov.in and keep the acknowledgement number.

  3. Tell your bank and block UPI

    Call the number on the back of your card or in your bank's official app. Ask them to block UPI and raise a fraud dispute.

  4. Save the evidence

    Take screenshots of messages, the scammer's number or UPI ID, and the transaction ID (UTR). Don't delete the chat.

  5. Secure your accounts

    Change your UPI PIN and passwords. Uninstall any screen-sharing or unknown app the scammer asked you to install.

  6. Report the scam number

    Report fraud calls and SMS on Sanchar Saathi (Chakshu) so the number can be blocked for others.

Remember these safety rules

🔐 Protect your PIN

Never share your UPI PIN or OTP with anyone, including "bank staff".

💸 Receiving money

You never need to enter your UPI PIN or approve a request to receive money.

📷 QR codes send money

Scanning a QR code is for paying. No one can send you money through a QR you scan.

🔎 Verify first

Check the recipient's name and verify unexpected requests through official apps.
